# Deactivate Allergen Category

## Pre-Condition

- None.

## Flow

1. Open the **Health > Configuration > Allergen Categories** menu.
2. Open the Allergen Category record to deactivate.
3. Click the **Edit** button.
4. Toggle the **Active** field off.
5. Click **Save**.

## Post-Condition

- The record is archived; an **Archived** ribbon appears on the form.
- The record no longer appears in the default list view.
- Deactivated Allergen Categories cannot be selected as an Allergen's **Category** in
  new records.
