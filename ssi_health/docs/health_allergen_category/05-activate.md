# Activate Allergen Category

## Pre-Condition

- The Allergen Category is archived (inactive).

## Flow

1. Open the **Health > Configuration > Allergen Categories** menu.
2. Enable the **Archived** filter in the search bar.
3. Open the archived Allergen Category record to reactivate.
4. Click the **Edit** button.
5. Toggle the **Active** field on.
6. Click **Save**.

## Post-Condition

- The record is restored and appears again in the default list view.
- The **Archived** ribbon no longer appears on the form.
- The Allergen Category can be selected again as an Allergen's **Category**.
