# Delete Disease Category

## Pre-Condition

- The Disease Category is not set as the **Parent Category** of another Disease
  Category.
- The Disease Category is not referenced by any Disease record.

## Flow

1. Open the **Health > Configuration > Disease Categories** menu.
2. Select one or more records to delete (check the checkbox).
3. Click **Action** > **Delete**.
4. Click **OK** to confirm.

## Post-Condition

- The selected Disease Category records are permanently removed from the system.
