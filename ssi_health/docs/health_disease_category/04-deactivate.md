# Deactivate Disease Category

## Pre-Condition

- None.

## Flow

1. Open the **Health > Configuration > Disease Categories** menu.
2. Open the Disease Category record to deactivate.
3. Toggle the **Active** field off.
4. Click **Save**.

## Post-Condition

- The record is archived; an **Archived** ribbon appears on the form.
- The record no longer appears in the default list view.
- Deactivated Disease Categories cannot be selected as a **Parent Category** or as a
  Disease's **Category** in new records.
