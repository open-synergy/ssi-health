# Activate Allergen

## Pre-Condition

- The Allergen is archived (inactive).

## Flow

1. Open the **Health > Configuration > Allergens** menu.
2. Enable the **Archived** filter in the search bar.
3. Open the archived Allergen record to reactivate.
4. Click the **Edit** button.
5. Toggle the **Active** field on.
6. Click **Save**.

## Post-Condition

- The record is restored and appears again in the default list view.
- The **Archived** ribbon no longer appears on the form.
- The Allergen can be selected again in new records.
